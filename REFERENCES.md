# References and attribution

1. Joel Brewster Lewis and Alejandro H. Morales,
   *Garsia–Remmel q-rook numbers are not always unimodal*,
   [arXiv:2410.19714v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.19714v3).
   Definitions on pages 1–2 and the final question of Remark 6 on page 5 specify
   the near-full problem. Proposition 4 concerns non-unimodality at other ranks.
2. Juan C. Migliore, Rosa M. Miró-Roig, and Uwe Nagel,
   *Monomial ideals, almost complete intersections and the Weak Lefschetz Property*,
   [arXiv:0811.1023v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.1023v2),
   Transactions of the American Mathematical Society 363 (2011), 229–257.
   Theorem 4.3 supplies the WLP counterexample family discussed in the manuscript;
   it is not a premise of the unimodality proof.
3. Bek Chase and Filip Jonsson Kling,
   *Monomial Almost Complete Intersections and the Strong Lefschetz Property*,
   [arXiv:2507.18516v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18516v1).
   The manuscript PDF uses the title *The strong Lefschetz property for some
   monomial almost complete intersections*. Definition 2.1, Theorem 3.1,
   Lemma 3.3, and Proposition 4.2 provide background, not the unrestricted
   nested-box proof.
4. Unpublished preliminary research notes supplied the product-difference
   reduction, monomial Hilbert-series interpretation, and finite computations.
   Those mathematical observations are credited here and derived in full in
   the manuscript. The notes themselves are not part of this distribution.

The proof was developed with AI assistance. No named external author is credited
with reviewing or endorsing this manuscript. This reference list does not
establish an exhaustive literature search or global priority.
